STRUCTURAL RIGID DESIGNS

Bespoke Structural Packaging Styles

Custom-engineered structures tailored to your product geometry, weight, and retail presentation.

Magnetic Flap Presentation Boxes

Concealed neodymium magnetic closures that snap shut with a satisfying acoustic click. Built on 1200–1800 GSM imported Kappa greyboard with velvet-laminated wraps.

Ideal for: Luxury Watches, Fragrances, VIP Corporate Gifting

Sliding Drawer (Matchbox) Boxes

A smooth sliding inner tray fitted with an artisanal grosgrain ribbon pull. Perfect for ceremonial jewellery, confectionery, artisanal chocolates, and cosmetic sets.

Ideal for: Jewellery, Artisanal Chocolates, Skincare Sets

Shoulder & Neck (Two-Piece) Boxes

Features an exposed contrasting inner neck (often wrapped in mirror gold or silver foil paper) that separates the lid and base, creating a distinct two-tone luxury profile.

Ideal for: Premium Spirits, Electronics, High-Fashion Accessories

Mono Cartons & Product Sleeves

High-volume folding cartons crafted on 300–450 GSM CyberXl Pac or FBB board with crash-lock bottom or tuck-in flaps. Enhanced with Raised 3D Spot UV and micro-embossing.

Ideal for: Cosmetics, Pharmaceuticals, FMCG Retail Displays

PACKAGING FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide a physical sample before bulk production?

Yes. For all custom rigid box orders, we build a 1:1 unprinted structural dummy or digital pre-press sample so you can verify product fit, magnet closure strength, and board thickness before bulk manufacturing.

Do you deliver across all areas of Mumbai?

Yes. Our facility in Saki Naka provides direct studio van and express courier deliveries to BKC, Lower Parel, Nariman Point, Andheri, Navi Mumbai, and Thane. We also dispatch securely nationwide.
