STANDARDS OF EXCELLENCE

Every Card, Built Right

We engineer our cards for structural density, razor-clean edges, and flawless optical clarity.

350 / 400 GSM Art Card

High-density, structurally rigid art card stock for crisp edge integrity and substantial in-hand weight. Strictly zero flimsy commercial stock.

Velvet or Matt Lamination

Choice between ultra-smooth Velvet Soft-Touch (sensory peach skin feel) or elegant glare-free Satin Matt protection on both faces.

Precision Cutting

Razor-sharp industrial guillotine 90° trim included as standard. Optional precision corner rounding (Corner Cut +) for sleek modern ergonomics.

Free Digital Pre-Press Proof

Our Mumbai pre-press technicians inspect bleeds, vector curves, and foil layers with a 1:1 proof before running a single production sheet.
