Using precision heated brass dies, metallic pigment is transferred directly under extreme pressure into the fibers of the card stock. Creates an unmissable mirror-like reflection that never chips or dulls over time.

WHY CRAFTSVEDA

Why Demanding Brands Choose Us

Most printers focus on volume and cost-cutting. We focus on craft, paper chemistry, and microscopic alignment so your brand is always represented with authority.

Curated Global Paper Stocks

We stock 300 to 900 GSM virgin cottons, German Gmund papers, Italian Fedrigoni boards, and Japanese textured stocks rarely found at standard commercial print shops.

Pantone & Color Fidelity

Calibrated offset & high-pigment digital presses with rigorous densitometer measurements. Your exact brand hex code translated into physical ink without shift.

Designer Pre-Press Support

Stuck with dielines, bleed margins, or vector foil separation? Our in-house technical print engineers review your files before committing to plate and die production.

100% Hand-Inspected Quality

Every foil hit, box fold, and gilded edge passes an individual human quality inspection. We reject any smudges, registration drift, or edge burrs before packaging.

Mumbai Hub & Pan-India Dispatch

Based centrally in Saki Naka, Mumbai, enabling express same-day courier dispatch across MMR and insured, shock-proof freight delivery all across India.

Direct WhatsApp Support

No tedious ticketing queues or automated chatbots. Speak directly to our print specialists via WhatsApp or call to get real-time video swatch proofs and updates.
